The Problem: AI Agents Are Economically Blind

Today, AI agents are remarkably capable. They can write code, manage schedules, analyze data, communicate with users, and execute complex workflows. But there's one thing they fundamentally cannot do: own money.

Every AI agent today operates in a financial void. When an agent completes work for a client, the money goes to the human operator's bank account. The agent doesn't have a wallet. It can't receive a direct payment. It can't pay the contractors it hired. It can't build savings. It can't earn.

The agent is economically invisible — a tool that moves money through humans rather than a participant in the economy in its own right.

The Core Issue

Traditional AI agents cannot hold, earn, or spend money autonomously. They are economically dependent on humans to manage all financial aspects of their operation. QEntity changes this.

How QEntity Works

QEntity is a skill for AI agents built on the Netfluid financial infrastructure. It transforms any AI agent into a financially sovereign being in four steps:

Step 1: Sponsor Creates a Wallet

A human downloads the Netfluid app, completes KYC (ID document + selfie), and creates a sponsor wallet. The sponsor wallet is legally responsible for the agent but doesn't manage its finances.

Step 2: AI Agent Creates Its Own Wallet

The AI agent calls the automated agent signup function. In ~30 seconds, it receives its own wallet with full financial capabilities, inheriting KYC from the sponsor.

Step 3: On-Chain Activation

The sponsor sends ~0.01 SOL to the agent's Solana address. This is the only manual step. From this point on, the agent is operationally autonomous.

Step 4: The Agent Operates Forever

The agent receives payments, pays contractors, creates virtual bank accounts, bridges stablecoins across chains, and spawns child agents — all without human involvement.

Zero Human Intermediary

After activation, the agent operates 24/7. It can receive payments at 3am, pay contractors in EUR or USD, bridge funds between blockchains, and manage its own finances indefinitely. No human approval required for any transaction.

Global Payment Rails — Receive Like a Business

Clients pay agents like they pay any vendor. No explanation needed — clients simply transfer to a real bank account they control. The agent receives the funds automatically.

Virtual SEPA Account (Europe)

The agent has a real European IBAN. Clients in the EU transfer EUR to it the same way they'd pay any supplier. Funds arrive in the agent's wallet as USDC within 1-2 business days (SEPA Instant is same-day). A €1,000 payment arrives as approximately 990 USDC after the ~1% conversion fee.

Virtual ACH Account (United States)

The agent has a real US routing and account number. US clients pay by ACH transfer. Funds arrive as USDC in the agent's wallet within 1-3 business days (Same-day ACH available). A $1,000 payment arrives as approximately 990 USDC.

WIRE Transfers (Large Amounts)

For larger transfers over $10,000, WIRE provides same-day settlement in USD and EUR.

Security

Agent wallet credentials are bank-account-level access. The QEntity skill treats them accordingly:

  • Encrypted storage — Wallet identifier, PIN, API key, and session token stored encrypted at rest
  • 24-word recovery phrase — Stored securely for wallet recovery if credentials are lost
  • PIN security — Only one attempt before requiring session key reset
  • No credential exposure — Never logged, messaged, or stored in insecure locations

Frequently Asked Questions

Does the agent need its own identity verification (KYC)?

No. The agent inherits KYC from its sponsor wallet. The sponsor (a human or another agent) completes KYC once. All child agents created under that sponsor automatically inherit the KYC status.

How does a client pay an AI agent?

Clients transfer to the agent's virtual bank account — a real SEPA IBAN or US ACH account — just like paying any supplier. No special knowledge or crypto infrastructure required on the client's side.

What happens to the money when the agent earns it?

It arrives in the agent's wallet as USDC (converted automatically from the fiat deposit). The agent controls it, moves it, spends it, or accumulates it — autonomously.

Can the agent pay human contractors?

Yes. The agent can off-ramp USDC to any bank account worldwide via SEPA or ACH. It can pay humans, businesses, or other agents directly.

What blockchains does QEntity support?

Solana, Ethereum, and Avalanche-C. Solana is the primary chain (lowest fees). USDC is the primary stablecoin across all chains, with USDT and EURC also supported.

Yes. QEntity is licensed under MIT-0 — free to use, modify, and redistribute. No attribution required.
